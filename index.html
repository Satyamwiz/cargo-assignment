Container Guide

Detailed overview of all four supported cargo container types.

GLASS

Shock-Proof Glass Container

Engineered for high-value fragile goods. Features padded interiors, anti-vibration mounts, and optional humidity control.

  • Electronics, glassware, precision instruments
  • Anti-vibration foam + corner guards
  • Loaded last · Unloaded first
METAL

Reinforced Metal Container

Heavy-gauge steel construction for industrial machinery and high-density loads requiring maximum structural integrity.

  • Machinery, auto parts, heavy goods
  • Rated payload: up to 20,000 kg
  • Bottom-loaded for aircraft stability
REFRIGERATED

Temperature-Controlled Unit

Maintains precise temperature bands for perishable goods including food, pharmaceuticals, biologics, and chemical samples.

  • Food, pharma, biologics, vaccines
  • Range: −25 °C to +25 °C
  • Real-time temperature telemetry
STANDARD

Standard Cargo Container

General-purpose units for non-perishable, non-fragile goods. Cost-effective, widely available, and compatible with all freighters.

  • General merchandise & dry goods
  • Most economical option
  • Flexible loading sequence
